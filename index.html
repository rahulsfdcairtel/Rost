THE ROST METHOD

We didn't reinvent coffee. We slowed it down.

Hot extraction is violent. We let time do the work — a sixteen-hour cold steep that pulls sweetness and chocolate while leaving the bitterness behind. The result is impossibly smooth, with none of the acidity.
